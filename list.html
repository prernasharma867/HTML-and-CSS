mythical creatures

dragon
a mythical creature A dragon is a legendary, serpentine, or reptilian creature. Across global folklore, these awe-inspiring beasts are typically described as massive, scaly monsters equipped with razor-sharp claws, immense bat-like wings, and the terrifying ability to breathe fire or magical energy.
phoenix
a mythical bird that cyclically regenerates or is otherwise born again. Associated with the sun, a phoenix obtains new life by arising from the ashes of its predecessor.The phoenix is a legendary, immortal bird from Greek and Egyptian mythology that symbolizes renewal, resurrection, and the sun. It is universally famous for its incredible life cycle: at the end of its life, the bird bursts into flames and is reborn from its own ashes.
vampire
a mythical being that feeds on the blood of the living. Vampires are often depicted as undead creatures with sharp teeth and claws, and they are said to be able to transform into bats or other animals.A vampire is a mythological undead creature that subsists by feeding on the vital life essence (typically blood) of the living. Over centuries, the lore has evolved from the bloated, feral corpses of Eastern European folklore into the pale, charismatic, and aristocratic beings seen in modern pop culture.
werewolf
a mythical creature that can transform from a human into a wolf or wolf-like beast. Werewolves are often associated with the full moon and are said to be able to control their transformations under certain conditions.A werewolf is a mythical shapeshifter—either a human who involuntarily transforms into a wolf-like beast or a human-wolf hybrid. Deeply rooted in folklore and often associated with the full moon and a vulnerability to silver, the concept dates back to ancient mythology and remains a staple of modern horror.
